# NovaCart Employee Handbook

NovaCart is a fictional online grocery delivery company founded in 2019 in Pune, India. It was started by Meera Kulkarni and Arjun Rao. The company delivers groceries within 30 minutes in 12 Indian cities.

## Company Basics

### Headquarters and Offices
The headquarters is in Pune, in the Hinjewadi tech park. NovaCart also has regional offices in Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Delhi. The Bengaluru office hosts the engineering team of about 140 people.

### Core Values
NovaCart has four core values: Customer First, Own It, Move Fast, and Be Kind. Every employee is reviewed on these four values during the yearly performance review.

## Working Hours and Leave

### Working Hours
Office hours are 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees can work from home on Wednesdays and Fridays. Core overlap hours, when everyone must be reachable, are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

### Annual Leave
Every full-time employee gets 24 paid leave days per year. Leave is credited monthly at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused leave days can be carried over to the next year. Any leave beyond 10 days expires on 31 March.

### Sick Leave
Employees get 8 sick leave days per year, separate from annual leave. A medical certificate is required if sick leave is longer than 2 consecutive days.

### Parental Leave
Mothers get 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Fathers get 2 weeks of paid paternity leave. Adoptive parents get the same leave as biological parents.

## Salary and Benefits

### Salary Payment
Salary is paid on the last working day of every month. Payslips are available on the HR portal called "NovaHub" by the 28th of each month.

### Health Insurance
NovaCart gives every employee a health insurance cover of 5 lakh rupees for the employee, spouse and up to 2 children. Parents can be added by paying an extra premium of 6,000 rupees per year.

### Learning Budget
Each employee gets a learning budget of 40,000 rupees per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of purchase with a receipt.

### Referral Bonus
If an employee refers a candidate who is hired and stays for 6 months, the employee gets a referral bonus of 25,000 rupees. For senior engineering roles the bonus is 50,000 rupees.

## Expenses and Travel

### Travel Policy
Domestic flights must be booked at least 7 days in advance, in economy class. Hotel stay is capped at 5,000 rupees per night in Tier 1 cities and 3,500 rupees per night in other cities.

### Daily Food Allowance
During business travel, the food allowance is 800 rupees per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed under any circumstances.

### Expense Claims
Expense claims must be filed on NovaHub within 15 days of the trip. Claims above 10,000 rupees need approval from the department head.

## Technology and Security

### Laptops
New employees receive a laptop on the first day. Engineers get a MacBook Pro, and all other employees get a Dell Latitude. Laptops are replaced every 3 years.

### Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must be changed every 90 days. Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the VPN and the code repository.

### Reporting a Security Incident
If an employee loses a laptop or suspects a data leak, they must email security@novacart.example within 1 hour. Delay in reporting can lead to disciplinary action.

## Delivery Operations

### Delivery Promise
NovaCart promises delivery within 30 minutes. If the delivery is late by more than 10 minutes, the customer gets a 50 rupee wallet credit automatically.

### Dark Stores
Orders are packed at small warehouses called dark stores. Each dark store covers a radius of 3 kilometres and stocks about 4,000 products. NovaCart currently runs 85 dark stores.

### Delivery Partner Rules
Delivery partners must wear a helmet and a NovaCart uniform. Partners are not allowed to ride faster than 40 km/h inside city limits.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I apply for leave?
Open NovaHub, go to the Leave section, pick the dates and submit. Your manager gets a notification and should respond within 2 working days.

### What is the notice period?
The notice period is 60 days for all employees. Employees in their first 6 months have a notice period of 30 days.

### Who do I contact for HR issues?
Write to hr@novacart.example or message the HR helpdesk on NovaHub. HR replies within 1 working day.
